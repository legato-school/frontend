# LegatoSchool - Product Overview

## Company

**LegatoSchool** is a music education platform that connects students with qualified piano and cello instructors for personalized lessons.

## Core Services

### 1. Music Lesson Scheduling

Streamlined booking system for music lessons:

- **Piano Lessons** - Classical, jazz, contemporary styles for all skill levels
- **Cello Lessons** - Classical technique, ensemble preparation, solo repertoire
- **Flexible Scheduling** - Online calendar integration with instructor availability
- **Lesson Management** - Progress tracking, practice assignments, recital preparation

### 2. Instructor Network

Curated network of qualified music educators:

- **Certified Instructors** - Verified credentials and teaching experience
- **Specialized Teaching** - Age-appropriate methods for children and adults
- **Performance Preparation** - Audition coaching, competition prep, recitals
- **Theory Integration** - Music theory woven into practical instruction

## Target Audience

- Students of all ages seeking quality piano or cello instruction
- Parents looking for reliable music education for their children
- Adult learners wanting to start or continue their musical journey
- Musicians preparing for auditions, competitions, or performances

## Value Proposition

- Easy-to-use scheduling platform for busy families
- Vetted, qualified instructors with proven teaching methods
- Flexible lesson formats (in-person, online, or hybrid)
- Progress tracking and performance opportunities

## Website Goals

1. Showcase instructor expertise and teaching philosophy
2. Provide easy lesson booking and scheduling
3. Build trust through student testimonials and success stories
4. Offer educational resources and practice tips
